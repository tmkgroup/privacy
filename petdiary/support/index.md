Need help with **Pet Diary**? Email **TMK Tech Group** at tmktechgroup@gmail.com. Please include your
device model, the app version and what happened.

## No account, no sign-in

Pet Diary works on your device. There is nothing to register and nothing to log in to. Everything you
add is stored on the phone itself — see the [privacy policy](/petdiary/) for what that means.

## Adding a pet

Open the **Pets** tab and tap **Add pet**. Only a name is required; species, breed, sex, birthday,
photo and the rest can be filled in later by opening the pet and tapping edit.

## Records inside a pet

Open a pet to find its tabs: overview, feeding, weight (with a chart), health (vaccinations, vet visits,
appointments, medicines, allergies, conditions, surgeries, lab tests, incidents), activity, care,
breeding (matings, litters, heat cycles) and documents & photos. Tap **Add** on a tab to write a new
entry, or tap an entry to edit it.

Health entries are copied from what your veterinarian tells you. Pet Diary does not suggest medicines,
doses, vaccine schedules or diagnoses.

## Reminders

Vaccination due dates, appointments, due dates and your own reminders are delivered by your phone, so
they work offline. If none arrive:

- allow notifications for Pet Diary in your device settings;
- on Android, also allow **alarms and reminders** (exact alarms) if your phone asks for it;
- battery savers on some Android phones delay alarms — exclude Pet Diary from the battery optimiser.

## Backup and moving to a new phone

**Settings → Back up** writes an encrypted `.tmkbak` file protected by a password you choose. On the new
phone, install Pet Diary and use **Settings → Restore from backup** with the same password. Keep the
password safe: without it the file cannot be opened.

## Ads and Remove ads

The free version shows ads. Watch a rewarded ad to turn ads off for 24 hours, or subscribe to
**Remove ads** to turn them off for as long as the subscription is active. Manage or cancel the
subscription in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
