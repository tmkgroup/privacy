Need help with **Food: Easy Recipes**? Email **TMK Tech Group** at contact@tmktechgroup.com. Please
include your device model, the app version from **Profile → About**, and what happened.

## No account, no sign-in

The App works on your device. There is nothing to register and nothing to log in to. Everything you
add is stored on the phone itself — see the [privacy policy](/food/) for what that means.

## Recipes and meal plans

The **Recipes** tab lists dishes by main ingredient. Open a dish for ingredients and steps. The
**Tools** tab has the meal planner, cook mode with timer, recipes from what is in your fridge, saved
recipes, water reminders, weight and BMI, and the other tools.

## Vegetarian days and reminders

**Tools → Vegetarian days** sets the lunar days of the month you eat vegetarian (the 1st and 15th, or
your own days) and reminds you on those days at the time you choose. Reminders are delivered by your own phone, so they work
offline. If none arrive, allow notifications for the App in your device settings.

## Food journal, diary and notes

The **Food journal** tab keeps what you eat. The diary and notes are in the **Tools** tab.

## Weather and nearby restaurants

The weather screen and the nearby restaurants map ask for your location while you use them. If you
refuse, pick a place yourself; the rest of the App works without location.

## Backups

**Menu → Backup & Restore** exports all your records to a `.json` file and restores it on another device. Keep the
file somewhere you trust: it is not encrypted.

## Ads and the Remove ads subscription

The free version shows a banner. Two ways to switch ads off:

- **Watch an ad** — one rewarded ad turns ads off for 24 hours.
- **Remove ads** — an auto-renewing subscription billed every 3 months, on the **Profile** screen.
  Tap **Restore purchases** on a new device. Manage or cancel it in your App Store or Google Play
  subscription settings; cancelling keeps it running to the end of the paid period.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the **Profile** screen has a button that reopens Google's consent
form so you can change your answer.

## Health content

Health-related dishes and tips are general reference only and do not replace a doctor.

## Something looks wrong

Tell us what you expected and what you saw, with the app version and a screenshot if you can:
contact@tmktechgroup.com.
