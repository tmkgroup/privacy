Need help with **Chinese Calendar**? Email **TMK Tech Group** at [tmktechgroup@gmail.com](mailto:tmktechgroup@gmail.com). Please include your device model, the region selected in the app (China, Hong Kong, Macao or Taiwan), and what happened.

## Holiday dates

Public holidays come from the official holiday notices of each government (gov.cn, gov.hk, 1823.gov.hk, gov.mo, dgpa.gov.tw, law.moj.gov.tw). If a date looks wrong for your region, email us with a link to the official notice.

## Remove ads subscription

- "Remove ads" is a 3-month subscription that renews automatically until you cancel. Find it in the **Me** tab under **Remove ads**.
- Bought it on another device, or reinstalled the app? Tap **Restore purchases** in the same section while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account.
- To cancel on iPhone or iPad: follow Apple's guide [Cancel a subscription from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428).
- To cancel on Android: follow Google's guide [Cancel, pause, or change a subscription on Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481). Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.

## Your data and backups

Your events, diary, notes, family tree and budget are saved only on your device. To move them to a new phone, use **Me → Export Backup**, then **Import Backup** on the new phone with the same passphrase. If you lose the passphrase, the backup cannot be opened.

## Language

Open **Me → Language** to switch between Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and English.

## Privacy

Read our [Privacy Policy](https://privacy.tmktechgroup.com/chinesecalendar/).
