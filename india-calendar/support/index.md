## No account, no sign-in

India Calendar works on your device. There is nothing to register and nothing to log in to. Your
profiles, Kundli, notes and settings are stored on the phone itself — see the
[privacy policy](/india-calendar/) for details.

## Choosing your location

On first launch the app asks for your location so that sunrise, sunset, Rahu Kalam and Muhurat are
calculated for your place. You can allow GPS, pick a city from the list, or use the default
(New Delhi). Change it any time in **Settings → Location** or from the Home screen.

If GPS does not work, allow location for India Calendar in your device settings, or simply pick a city.

## Notifications and reminders

Daily Panchang, Rahu Kalam, festival, vrat and event reminders are scheduled by your own phone, so they
work offline. If none arrive:

- allow notifications for India Calendar in your device settings;
- on Android, also allow **alarms and reminders** (exact alarms) if your phone asks for it;
- battery savers on some Android phones delay alarms — exclude India Calendar from the battery optimiser.

## Backup and restore

**Settings → Backup & Restore**:

- **Export** asks for a passphrase, then writes an encrypted `.tmkbak` file and opens the share sheet
  so you can keep it wherever you like — your own cloud drive, email or a computer.
- **Import** reads such a file back after you type the same passphrase.

**Write the passphrase down.** The file is encrypted with it (AES-256-GCM); if it is lost, nobody can
open the backup, including us.

## Ads and the Remove ads subscription

The free version shows ads. **Remove ads** is an auto-renewing subscription, US$1.00 per 3 months,
opened from **Settings**. Tap **Restore** on a new device. Manage or cancel it in your App Store or
Google Play subscription settings; cancelling keeps it running to the end of the paid period.

## Language and theme

**Settings** has the app language, light / dark / follow-system mode and the accent colour.

Tell us what you expected and what you saw, with the app version and a screenshot if you can:
tmktechgroup@gmail.com. We read every message.
