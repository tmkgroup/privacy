Need help with **Xiangqi Clash**? Email **TMK Tech Group** at [tmktechgroup@gmail.com](mailto:tmktechgroup@gmail.com). Please include your device model and what happened.

## Remove ads subscription

- "Remove ads" is a 3-month subscription that renews automatically until you cancel. While it is active the game shows no ads, and hints and undos that normally need an ad are free.
- Bought it on another device, or reinstalled the game? Open **Settings → Restore purchases** while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account.
- To cancel on iPhone or iPad: follow Apple's guide [Cancel a subscription from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428).
- To cancel on Android: follow Google's guide [Cancel, pause, or change a subscription on Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481). Uninstalling the game does not cancel the subscription.

## Language

Open **Settings → Language** to choose one of 72 languages.

## Your progress

Your settings, progress and game history are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game removes them.

## Privacy

Read our [Privacy Policy](https://privacy.tmkgroup.vn/xiangqiclash/).
