## Summary

**Scan Doc works on your device. We collect nothing.**

There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash
reporter and no backend of ours. Pages you scan, the text that recognition
produces, the PDF, Word and Markdown files the app writes, your signature and
your watermarks all stay in Scan Doc's own folder on your phone.

If you uninstall Scan Doc, every byte of that is gone with it. We cannot recover
anything for you, and neither can anyone else, because no copy ever left your
device.

The one time Scan Doc uses the network is when you translate text. Google's
on-device translation needs a language pack, and the app asks Google to download
it the first time you pick that language. The text you are translating is not
sent anywhere.

## Data we collect

### From you, automatically

**None.** Scan Doc has no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting and no
server of ours. We have no way to identify you, see what you scan, or know that
you exist as a user.

### From you, with your consent

**Camera** is used only while you are scanning a document or an ID card, to
photograph the pages in front of you. Deny it and the rest of the app still
works.

**Photo library** is read only when you tap Import images and pick pictures
yourself. The app receives the pictures you selected and nothing else.

**Files you pick** reach the app one at a time through the system file picker,
when you choose a PDF or an image for the PDF tools. The app never browses your
storage on its own.

You can revoke any permission in your device settings at any time. The app keeps
working with fewer features.

### From your device, locally

Stored inside Scan Doc's own application folder and settings: the page images
the scanner produced and pictures you imported; recognised text for each
document; document records in a local database; files the app writes for you
(PDF, Word, Markdown, page images, your signature, watermarked pages); and your
settings (light or dark mode, accent colour, app language, writing system for
text recognition).

This data is never uploaded anywhere. It is yours, and only yours.

## Third-party services

**Google ML Kit**, on-device, does three jobs in the app: the document scanner
that finds the edges of the page, text recognition for Latin, Chinese,
Devanagari, Japanese and Korean script, and translation of the recognised text.

ML Kit does its work on your device: page images and recognised text are not
sent to Google for processing. Two things do travel over the network, both
triggered by you - the translation language pack download, once per language,
and on Android the scanning and recognition components themselves, which Google
Play services fetches onto the phone the first time they are used. Those
requests go to Google, under Google's own privacy policy. They carry no document
of yours.

There is no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Sentry, AdMob,
Facebook SDK, or any other telemetry or advertising endpoint in Scan Doc.

When you tap Share, the app opens the standard system share sheet. Whatever you
send goes where you direct it, not to TMK Group.

## Data retention

On-device data is kept until you delete the document in the app or uninstall
Scan Doc. There is no off-device data held by us. A file you shared or saved
elsewhere stays wherever you put it, for as long as you keep it; delete it from
that location to remove it. We never had a copy.

## Children's privacy

Scan Doc is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal data and
operate no server, the app cannot tell the age of any user. We do not knowingly
collect data from children under 13, or 16 in the EU; we do not collect data
from anyone.

## Your rights

You have full control because the data lives on your device. Every document,
page and recognised text is visible inside the app. Export PDF, Export Word, To
Markdown and Share take any document out of it. Deleting a document from its
detail screen removes it, and uninstalling the app removes everything. You can
edit the recognised text, rename, rotate, reorder, add and remove pages
directly inside the app. Revoking a permission in your device settings leaves
the app working with reduced features.

You may also contact tmktechgroup@gmail.com with any question, but we cannot
delete what we never had.

## Changes to this policy

We will update the "Last updated" date above and ship a new app version whenever
this policy changes.
