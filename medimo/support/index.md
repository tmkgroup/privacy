Need help with **Medimo**? Email **TMK Tech Group** at [tmktechgroup@gmail.com](mailto:tmktechgroup@gmail.com). Please include your device model and what happened.

## Not a medical device

Medimo helps you keep health information organised for yourself and your family. It does not diagnose, does not treat and does not replace a licensed doctor. Always ask a health professional about anything medical.

## No account, no sign-in

Medimo works offline. There is nothing to register and nothing to log in to, so there is no password to lose and no server of ours that can go down.

## Your records live on your device

Profiles, visits, prescriptions, vaccinations, vitals, documents and photos of papers are kept in encrypted on-device storage. Uninstalling Medimo deletes all of it, and nobody — including us — can recover it.

## Family profiles

Add a profile for everyone in your household from the dashboard. Each profile keeps its own records, vaccination schedule, reminders and charts, and the dashboard follows whichever profile you are looking at.

## Vaccinations and reminders

The vaccination screens hold the children's schedule and adult boosters, and can remind you before the next dose. Medication reminders work the same way. Allow notifications when Medimo asks; reminders are scheduled by your phone, so they arrive with no network. Denying the permission leaves the rest of the app working normally.

## Vitals, wellness and charts

Record blood pressure, glucose, weight, temperature, SpO2 and heart rate, plus activity, water, sleep, nutrition, mood, cycle and child growth. The charts and insights screens read back what you entered.

## Apple Health and Bluetooth devices

Health sync reads steps, sleep and heart rate from Apple Health after you grant it in the iOS Health app. Bluetooth medical devices that follow the standard GATT profiles (blood-pressure monitors, glucometers, smart scales) can be paired from the Bluetooth screen. Both are optional.

## Documents, text from photos and QR codes

Photograph a prescription or a lab sheet and Medimo reads the text from the picture on the device. The QR scanner imports vaccination certificates and health-insurance cards. The camera is used only while a screen like this is open.

## Emergency Medical ID

The emergency card shows blood type, allergies, chronic conditions, current medication and a contact, for a first responder to read on the lock screen of your choice.

## Backups

Open **Settings → Backup** to export an encrypted backup file protected by a passphrase only you know, and to restore it on a new phone. Without the passphrase the file cannot be opened, by you or by us. Restoring replaces what is in the app, and Medimo asks you to confirm first.

## App lock

**Settings → Security** turns on a PIN or Face ID lock, asked each time the app opens. There is no way for us to reset it, so if you forget it you will need to reinstall and restore from a backup.

## Ads and Remove ads

Medimo is free and shows a banner. Watching one rewarded video turns ads off for 24 hours. **Remove ads** (US$1.00 / 3 months, auto-renewing) turns them off for good — open **Settings → Remove ads** for the store price, the renewal notice and **Restore purchases**. A subscription renews until you cancel it in your store account settings. Terms of Use (EULA): <https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/>

## Language and appearance

**Settings** holds Theme (System / Light / Dark), the accent colour and Language.
