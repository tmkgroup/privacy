Need help with **Garden Log**? Email **TMK Tech Group** at tmktechgroup@gmail.com. Please include your
device model, the app version from **Settings → About**, and what happened.

## No account, no sign-in

Garden Log works on your device. There is nothing to register and nothing to log in to, so there is no
password to lose and no server that can go down. Everything you add is stored on the phone itself —
see the [privacy policy](/gardenlog/) for what that means.

## Adding your first plant

Open the **Plants** tab and tap the **+** button. Give the plant a name, and optionally a species, a
photo, the place it grows in and the date you planted it. The plant then appears on **Today** as soon
as it has something due.

Nothing is required except a name: you can fill the rest in later by opening the plant and tapping
edit.

## Care schedules and reminders

Open a plant and add a schedule — watering, fertilising, pruning, repotting — with the interval that
suits it. **Today** lists what is due now and **Upcoming (7 days)** what is coming. Tap **Done** on a
task and it is logged and rescheduled.

Reminders are delivered by your own phone, so they work offline. If none arrive:

- allow notifications for Garden Log in your device settings;
- on Android, also allow **alarms and reminders** (exact alarms) if your phone asks for it;
- battery savers on some Android phones delay alarms — exclude Garden Log from the battery optimiser.

## Skipping a watering when rain is coming

If you let the app use your location, the weather card on **Today** shows the local forecast and a
watering task can be skipped when rain is expected. The forecast comes from Open-Meteo; no weather
account or key is needed.

No location? Pick a place on the map instead, and the card will use that.

## The timeline, diary and notes

**Timeline** shows every photo and logged action in date order, per plant or for the whole garden.
**Journal** is a free-form diary with mood and photos, and **Notes** is for quick jottings and
one-off reminders that are not tied to a plant.

## Harvest, expenses and insights

Log what you pick in **Harvest** with weight or count. **Thu chi / Spending** keeps what you spend on
seed, soil and tools. **Insights** then charts yield, costs and which plants are doing best.

## The Wiki tab

**Wiki** looks up plant articles from Wikipedia in the app's language. It needs a connection; nothing
you type is stored by us.

## Where to buy supplies

**Today → Quick access → Supply shops** opens a map of nearby garden-supply shops read from the map
data, filtered by fertiliser, pesticide and seedling sellers. The map centres on you once your phone
returns a position; this map layer only covers Vietnam, so outside Vietnam the screen says so instead
of showing an empty map.

## Identifying a plant or a disease with AI

The AI screen is off until you set it up with your **own** Google Gemini API key:

1. Get a free key at <https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey>.
2. In Garden Log, open **Settings → AI** and paste it.
3. Then use the AI button on the Plants screen to identify a plant or diagnose a leaf from a photo.

The key stays on your device, and the photo goes to Google under your own key. Remove the key and the
feature stops.

## Backups

**Settings → Backup & sync**:

- **Export encrypted backup** writes a `.tmkbak` file protected by a passphrase you choose. Keep it
  anywhere you like — your own cloud drive, a computer, a memory card.
- **Restore from backup** reads such a file back.
- **Automatic backup** keeps the most recent copies on the device itself, every few days.
- **CSV export** hands your records to a spreadsheet.

**Write the passphrase down.** The file is encrypted with it (AES-256-GCM); if it is lost, nobody can
open the backup, including us.

## Ads and the Remove ads subscription

The free version shows a banner. Two ways to switch ads off:

- **Watch an ad** — one rewarded ad turns ads off for 24 hours.
- **Remove ads** — an auto-renewing subscription, US$1.00 per 3 months, in **Settings → Remove ads**.
  Tap **Restore purchases** on a new device. Manage or cancel it in your App Store or Google Play
  subscription settings; cancelling keeps it running to the end of the paid period.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, **Settings → Ad privacy options** reopens Google's consent form so
you can change your answer.

## Language and theme

**Settings** has the app language, light / dark / follow-system mode and the accent colour. The app
follows your phone's language by default.
