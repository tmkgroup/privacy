Need help with **Taqwim**? Email **TMK Tech Group** at tmktechgroup@gmail.com. Please include your
device model, the app version and what happened.

## No account, no sign-in

Taqwim works on your device. There is nothing to register and nothing to log in to. Your prayer log,
notes, diary, bookmarks and Quran progress are stored on the phone itself — see the
[privacy policy](/taqwim/) for details.

## Prayer times look wrong

Prayer times depend on your place and on the calculation method of your country or mosque:

- **Settings → Location** — allow approximate location, or enter coordinates / pick a saved place by hand.
- **Settings → Calculation method** — Muslim World League, Umm al-Qura, Egyptian, Karachi, ISNA,
  Moonsighting Committee, Dubai, Qatar, Kuwait, Singapore, Turkey, Tehran and more.
- **Settings → Asr (madhab)** — Shafi / Maliki / Hanbali use one shadow length, Hanafi uses two.
- **Settings → High latitude rule** — for places where the sun does not set far enough in summer.
- **Settings → Adjustments** — add or subtract minutes per prayer to match your local mosque exactly.

## Notifications and the adhan

Adhan, pre-prayer reminders, iqamah, suhoor and iftar, Tahajjud, Jumu'ah, the daily verse and hadith
and eclipse alerts are scheduled by your own phone, so they work offline. If none arrive:

- allow notifications for Taqwim in your device settings;
- on Android, also allow **alarms and reminders** (exact alarms) if your phone asks for it;
- battery savers on some Android phones delay alarms — exclude Taqwim from the battery optimiser;
- a phone in silent or Do Not Disturb mode will not play the adhan sound.

## Qibla

The Qibla screen uses the phone's magnetometer. Keep the phone flat and away from metal, magnets and
car mounts; if the needle drifts, calibrate the compass by moving the phone in a figure-of-eight. The
AR Qibla screen needs camera permission; nothing it sees is recorded or sent anywhere.

## Mosques and halal places

The map shows mosques and halal places around you from our country lists, or from OpenStreetMap data
when your country has no list yet. Anything missing or wrong there comes from the map data — tell us
and we will look at it.

## Quran audio

The Quran text and translations are inside the app and work offline. Tapping **Play** on a verse
downloads that one audio file and keeps it for offline replay, so the first play of a verse needs a
connection.

## Backup and restore

**Settings → Backup & Restore**:

- **Export** asks for a passphrase, then writes an encrypted `.tmkbak` file and opens the share sheet
  so you can keep it wherever you like — your own cloud drive, email or a computer.
- **Import** reads such a file back after you type the same passphrase.

**Write the passphrase down.** The file is encrypted with it (AES-256-GCM); if it is lost, nobody can
open the backup, including us.

## Ads and the Remove ads subscription

The free version shows a banner. No feature is locked behind an ad; one optional rewarded ad hides the
banners for 24 hours. **Remove ads** is an auto-renewing subscription, US$1.00 per 3 months, opened
from **Settings**. Tap **Restore** on a new device. Manage or cancel it in your App Store or Google
Play subscription settings; cancelling keeps it running to the end of the paid period.

Tips are optional and unlock nothing — every feature is free.

## Language and theme

Taqwim is available in 16 languages, including right-to-left layouts for Arabic, Urdu, Persian, Pashto
and Punjabi. **Settings** has the app language, light / dark / follow-system mode and the accent colour.
