## Home Care: Warranty & Repairs — Support

Need help with Home Care? Email TMK Tech Group at
<tmktechgroup@gmail.com>. Please include your device model, your phone's
operating system version, and what happened.

## Reminders

Home Care reminds you 30 days, 7 days, 1 day and on the day itself before a
warranty expires, a maintenance job falls due, or a repair is ready for
pickup. Reminders fire at 9 a.m. local time and are built on your phone, so
they work without internet.

Not getting them? Allow notifications for Home Care in your phone's settings,
and on Android make sure battery optimisation is not stopping the app.

## Remove ads subscription

- "Remove ads" is a 3-month subscription that renews automatically until you
  cancel. Find it in **Settings → Ads**.
- You can also tap **Watch an ad** in the same place to turn ads off for
  24 hours, free.
- Bought it on another device, or reinstalled the app? Tap **Restore
  purchases** in the same section while signed in to the same Apple ID or
  Google account.
- To cancel on iPhone or iPad: follow Apple's guide
  [Cancel a subscription from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT202039).
- To cancel on Android: follow Google's guide
  [Cancel, pause, or change a subscription on Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481).
  Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.

## Your data and backups

Your devices, repairs, maintenance schedules, spending, diary and notes are
saved only on your phone. To move them to a new phone, use
**Settings → Backup** to export a backup file, then restore it on the new
phone. Keep the file somewhere safe — we have no copy of it.

The device list also exports to CSV, and a device's history exports to PDF,
from the same Settings screen.

## Repair shops and the map

Picking a repair shop's location opens a map. The map needs internet; your
position is only used to centre it and is never stored by us. You can also
paste coordinates copied from Google Maps instead of granting the location
permission.

## Smart home

Home Care can show a Home Assistant hub on your own network. Enter your hub's
address and access token in **Tools → Smart Home**; the app talks to the hub
directly and the token stays on your phone.

## Language

Open **Settings → Language** to change the language.
