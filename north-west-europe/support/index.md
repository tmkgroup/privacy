Need help with **Northwest Europe Calendar**? Email **TMK Tech Group** at [tmktechgroup@gmail.com](mailto:tmktechgroup@gmail.com). Please include your device model, the country selected in the app, and what happened.

## Holiday dates

Holiday dates come from the official public-holiday laws and government notices of each country. Open **Profile → About holiday dates** to see the sources. If a date looks wrong for your country, email us with a link to the official notice.

## Remove ads subscription

- "Remove ads" is a 3-month subscription that renews automatically until you cancel. Find it in **Profile → Remove ads**.
- Bought it on another device, or reinstalled the app? Tap **Restore purchases** in the same section while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account.
- To cancel on iPhone or iPad: follow Apple's guide [Cancel a subscription from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428).
- To cancel on Android: follow Google's guide [Cancel, pause, or change a subscription on Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481). Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.

## Your data and backups

Your events, diary, notes and budget are saved only on your device. To move them to a new phone, use **Profile → Backup → Export encrypted backup (.tmkbak)**, then **Restore from backup** on the new phone with the same passphrase. If you lose the passphrase, the backup cannot be opened.

## Language

Open **Profile → Language** to change the language.

## Privacy

Read our [Privacy Policy](https://privacy.tmktechgroup.com/north-west-europe/).
