<div markdown="1">

Need help with **TMK Land**? Email **trucmn@gmail.com**. Please include your device model, the account email you sign in with, and what happened.

## Signing in

Sign in with Apple or Google. Without signing in you can still browse listings, the Utilities screen and news.

## Listings

Create a listing with photos, location and price, browse listings on the map, save the ones you like and follow other users. Each listing's menu has **Report** and **Block user**.

## Rental management

Record rooms, tenants, contracts, deposits, rent receipts, incidents and contract settlement. Receipts are records only: rent is not paid through the app.

## Shared investment and the Trading center

In the **Assets** tab, co-owners record each person's contribution, schedule and agreed interest settings for a property they already own together. **Profile → Trading center** records when one co-owner passes part of a recorded share to someone else. The app only stores these numbers; no money moves through the app.

## Coins

Coins are an in-app item used to buy listing packages. They cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for cash.

## Ads

TMK Land shows no ads.

## Deleting your account

**Profile → Settings → Delete account.**

## Privacy

Read our [Privacy Policy](https://privacy.tmktechgroup.com/tmk_land/).
