Need help with **Supreme Dominion**? Email **TMK Tech Group** at [tmktechgroup@gmail.com](mailto:tmktechgroup@gmail.com). Please include your device model and what happened.

## Remove ads subscription

- "Remove ads" is a 3-month subscription that renews automatically until you cancel. You find it at the top of the Shop. While it is active the game shows no ads, and every "Watch" button gives its reward right away.
- Bought it on another device, or reinstalled the game? Open the **Shop → Restore purchases** while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account.
- To cancel on iPhone or iPad: follow Apple's guide [Cancel a subscription from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428).
- To cancel on Android: follow Google's guide [Cancel, pause, or change a subscription on Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481). Uninstalling the game does not cancel the subscription.

## Moving to a new phone (Cloud save)

1. On your old phone, open **Settings → Cloud save** and write down the backup code (10 letters and numbers) and the password (16).
2. On the new phone, open **Settings → Cloud save → Restore a backup** and type them in.

Keep the code and password private: anyone who has them can restore your save.

## Gift codes

Open **Settings → Gift codes** to type a code. The **News** button there shows the codes in use and the latest news.

## Language

Open **Settings → Language** to choose your language.

## Privacy

Read our [Privacy Policy](https://privacy.tmkgroup.vn/supremedominion/).
