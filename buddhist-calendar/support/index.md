Need help with **Buddhist Calendar+**? Email **TMK Tech Group** at tmktechgroup@gmail.com. Please include your
device model, the country you are using in the app, and what happened.

## No account, no sign-in

The app works on your device. There is nothing to register and nothing to log in to, so there is no
password to lose and no server that can go down. Everything you add is stored on the phone itself —
see the [privacy policy](/buddhist-calendar/) for what that means.

## Choosing your country

The first time you open the app it picks a country from your phone's region or language: Thailand, Laos,
Myanmar, Cambodia, Bhutan, Mongolia or Nepal. If it cannot match one, it shows the list so you can choose.

To change country later, open the profile tab and use **Change country**. Your diary, notes, budget and
"remove ads" purchase stay with you when you switch.

## The calendar of each country

Every country keeps its own traditional calendar: Thai, Lao, Burmese and Khmer lunar calendars with the
Buddhist era and the holy days (uposatha); the Tibetan Phugpa calendar for Bhutan and Mongolia; and the
Bikram Sambat calendar for Nepal. Public holidays, almanac readings and festival days are built into the
app, so the calendar works with no internet connection.

## Diary, notes, reminders and countdown

- **Diary** — one entry per day, kept on your device.
- **Notes** — write a note and attach a reminder; your phone shows it at the time you set.
- **Events and countdown** — add birthdays and anniversaries and see the days remaining.

If a reminder does not appear, check that notifications are allowed for the app in your phone settings,
and that battery saving is not stopping the app from running in the background.

## Household budget

The budget tool records income and expenses in the currency of your country and can import or export an
encrypted backup file. Exchange rates and market prices are fetched only after you agree to connect.

## Weather, sunrise and sunset

Allow location access and the Today screen shows your local forecast and the sunrise and sunset times for
where you are. If you refuse, the app uses a default city for your country — the app still works.

## Appearance

The profile tab has **Light / Dark / Follow system** and a row of main colours. The first circle is the
colour of the country you are using; pick any other to change the whole app.

## Games and ads

The free app shows a banner on the Tools and Games tabs and offers an optional rewarded ad for extra
hints in a game. No calendar feature is locked behind an ad.

## Remove ads

**Remove ads** is a subscription of 3 months that renews automatically until you cancel it. Buy it from the
profile tab. If you change phone or reinstall, open the same screen and tap **Restore purchases**. Cancel
any time in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

## Backup

Export an encrypted backup (`.tmkbak`) before you change phone, and import it on the new one. Keep the
passphrase: without it the file cannot be opened, and we cannot recover it for you.
