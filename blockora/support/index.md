Need help with **Blockora Block Puzzle**? Email **TMK Tech Group** at [tmktechgroup@gmail.com](mailto:tmktechgroup@gmail.com). Please include your device model and what happened.

## Remove Ads subscription

- "Remove Ads" is a 3-month subscription that renews automatically until you cancel. While it is active the game shows no banner or full-screen ads; videos you choose to watch for a reward stay available.
- Bought it on another device, or reinstalled the game? Open **Shop → Remove Ads → Restore your purchase** while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account.
- To cancel on iPhone or iPad: follow Apple's guide [Cancel a subscription from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428).
- To cancel on Android: follow Google's guide [Cancel, pause, or change a subscription on Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481). Uninstalling the game does not cancel the subscription.

## Your progress

Your settings, scores and progress are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Read our [Privacy Policy](https://privacy.tmktechgroup.com/blockora/).
