This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game **Blockora Block Puzzle** ("the App"), published by **TMK Tech Group** ("we", "us"), handles information. The App is not directed to children under 13.

## 1. Information we do not collect

- The App has no user accounts and no sign-in.
- We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location.

## 2. Information stored only on your device

The App saves the following on your device so it can remember your choices and progress: sound and vibration settings, your rotation count, scores and best scores, level and daily-challenge progress, unfinished games, and whether you have removed ads. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the App removes it.

## 3. Internet connections

The App does not send your game data to our servers. Some game modes may download optional event content over the internet; like any internet connection, the server delivering that content receives your device's IP address.

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob). According to Google, the SDK automatically collects and shares the following data for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention purposes:

- IP address (may be used to estimate the general location of the device)
- User product interactions (such as app launches, taps, and video views)
- Diagnostic information (performance of the App and the SDK)
- Device and account identifiers (such as the advertising ID and app set ID)

This data is encrypted in transit. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from the "Privacy options" button in Settings. On iPhone and iPad, the App asks for permission before using your advertising identifier. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

If you buy "Remove Ads", the App stops showing banner and full-screen ads. Videos you choose to watch for a reward stay available.

- Google's privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- How Google uses data from partner apps: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

## 5. In-app purchase

The App offers one optional purchase, "Remove Ads", a subscription that renews automatically every 3 months until you cancel. Payment is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we do not receive your payment details. The App only records on your device that ads have been removed, and the "Restore your purchase" button asks the store to confirm a previous purchase.

## 6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at the same address.

## 8. Contact
