Need help with **Fincore**? Email **TMK Tech Group** at [tmktechgroup@gmail.com](mailto:tmktechgroup@gmail.com). Please include your device model and what happened.

## No account, no sign-in

Fincore works fully offline. There is nothing to register and nothing to log in to, so there is no password to lose and no server that can go down.

## Your data lives on your device

Every transaction, category, note, budget and savings goal is kept in encrypted storage on your phone only. Uninstalling Fincore deletes all of it, and nobody — including us — can recover it.

## Backups

Open **More → Cloud backup** to reach the **Backup & Restore** screen. **Export Backup** writes an encrypted `.tmkbak` file you choose a passphrase for — keep both the file and the passphrase somewhere you control. **Restore Backup** reads that file back on a new phone, or after reinstalling. This is the only way to move your data between devices.

Without the passphrase the file cannot be opened, by you or by us. Restoring overwrites what is currently in the app, and Fincore asks you to confirm before it does.

## Payment reminders

Recurring entries (**More → Recurring**) can remind you before they fall due. Allow notifications when Fincore asks, then set how many days ahead you want the reminder in **More → Payment reminders**. Reminders are scheduled by your phone; nothing is sent to a server. Denying the permission leaves the rest of the app working normally.

## More than one currency

Each wallet (**More → Accounts**) keeps its own currency. Fincore **does not convert anything** — it reports each currency separately, so you can see how much you hold in your home currency and how much in foreign ones. When you transfer between two wallets kept in different currencies, type the amount that actually arrived in the **Amount received** field.

**Currency converter** and **Financial markets** under **More → Tools** are a separate lookup tool. They never touch your recorded transactions.

## PIN lock

Open **More → Security & PIN** to turn one on. It is asked for each time the app opens. There is no way for us to reset it, so if you forget it you will need to reinstall and restore from a backup.

## Language and appearance

The **Appearance** section of **More** holds **Theme** (System / Light / Dark), **Accent color** and **Language**. **Default currency** is there too — changing it does not convert anything already recorded.
