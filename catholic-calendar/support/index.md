Need help with **Catholic Calendar**? Email **TMK Tech Group** at [tmktechgroup@gmail.com](mailto:tmktechgroup@gmail.com). Please include your device model, the language selected in the app, and what happened.

## Nearby churches and Mass times

Church locations come from OpenStreetMap. Where OpenStreetMap does not record which church a building belongs to, the app says so on the church card instead of guessing. Mass times are missing for most churches because OpenStreetMap rarely records them — if you know the times for a church, open **Nearby Church → contribute Mass times** and send them to us with a photo of the notice board.

If a church near you is missing from the map, it is missing from OpenStreetMap; adding it at [openstreetmap.org](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) makes it appear in the app.

## Remove ads subscription

- "Remove ads" is a 3-month subscription that renews automatically until you cancel. Find it in **Profile → Remove ads**.
- Bought it on another device, or reinstalled the app? Tap **Restore purchases** in the same section while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account.
- To cancel on iPhone or iPad: follow Apple's guide [Cancel a subscription from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428).
- To cancel on Android: follow Google's guide [Cancel, pause, or change a subscription on Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481). Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.

## Your data and backups

Your prayer journal, notes, reminders, countdowns and budget are saved only on your device. To move them to a new phone, use **Profile → Backup data (encrypted)**, then **Restore from backup** on the new phone with the same passphrase. If you lose the passphrase, the backup cannot be opened.

## Language

Open **Profile → Language** to change the language.
